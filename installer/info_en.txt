NINA - Drumbox Sequencer Powerhouse

This installer contains everything you need: the stand-alone application (with ASIO,
WASAPI and DirectSound audio support), the VST3 plug-in for Cubase and other DAWs, the user
manual and the licences. You do not need to download anything else.

- ASIO: NINA can use the ASIO driver of your audio interface. The drivers themselves come
  from your audio hardware maker; without one, NINA plays through Windows audio (WASAPI).
